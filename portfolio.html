The problem behind the product

When every role needs a different version of your story, tailoring becomes a second job.

Too many directions.

Product, strategy, operations, legal, and everything adjacent can each require a different emphasis. A generic resume rarely tells the right story.

One anchor makes it useful.

Choose a target role and add one reference job. The prototype uses that anchor to focus the language, fit assessment, and preparation around a real opportunity.

From profile to point of view

A simple workflow designed around the decisions applicants actually make.

01

Bring your evidence

Add your background and upload a CV as the factual source of truth.

02

Pick a direction

Create a role profile for each path you are seriously considering.

03

Anchor the role

Paste a reference job so the tool learns the responsibilities and language that matter.

04

Make the case

Generate tailored materials, inspect gaps, and prepare for the interview.
